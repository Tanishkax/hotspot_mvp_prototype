    Raw MODIS, raw VIIRS and the harmonised record

    How the two sensors differ, how MODIS is rescaled to VIIRS-equivalent units, and how well that holds up on years the ratios never saw.

    Raw MODIS

    Terra (from 2000) and Aqua (from mid-2002), ~1 km pixels. Sees large, hot fires; misses many small ones. The only record before 2012.

    Raw VIIRS S-NPP

    375 m pixels from 2012. Detects several times more fires than MODIS over the same area. Used as the reference sensor.

    Harmonised

    One continuous series in VIIRS-equivalent units: VIIRS where available, MODIS × latitude-band ratio before. Never a sum of both sensors.
